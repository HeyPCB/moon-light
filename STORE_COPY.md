# Luna, the night-light moon

**Hook:** A sleepy moon that turns on at dusk and tucks itself in after you fall asleep.

**Description (60 words):**
Luna glows a soft candle-amber when the room gets dark, then fades out after 15, 45 or 90 minutes and
stays off until morning. No blue light to wake you, no switch to find in the dark: just pat its gold
cheek. Hang it from a nail by its keyhole or lay it on the nightstand. Powered by any USB-C.

**Suggested price:** $24

**Lifestyle photo ideas:**
1. A dim nursery wall: Luna hanging beside a crib mobile, its inner curve glowing amber, the rest of the
   room in deep blue.
2. A hand patting the gold cheek at bedtime, a book and a glass of water on the nightstand.
3. A long-exposure hallway shot at 3 a.m.: a child walking to the bathroom by Luna's glow.
